### 📌 Hypoglycaemic coma

- Blood glucose <55 mg/dL
### 📌 Diabetic ketoacidosis [DKA]

- Ketonaemia or Ketonuria [3+ or 4+]

- Blood glucose >250 mg/dL

- Metabolic acidosis [pH <7.30, HCO3 <20 mEq/L]

- Serum osmolarity >350 mOsm/kg
