First discovery in 1954

๏ Inherited autosomal recessive blood disorder characterised by 

abnormal formation of haemoglobin

- 97% of adult haemoglobin 
‣ HbA2 : 2 alpha and 2 delta chains 
- 1.5-3% of adult haemoglobin 
‣ HbF : 2 alpha and 2 gamma chains 

- 1% of adult haemoglobin 
- 70-90% at term, fall to 25% by 1st month and progressively
